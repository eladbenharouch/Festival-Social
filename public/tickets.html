    ⚠️ Ticket Resale Policy

    In accordance with Israeli law regarding ticket scalping, tickets for performances may not be offered for resale above their stated original price.

    Festival Social does not allow a selling price higher than the original ticket price entered by the seller.

    The seller declares that the original price entered is the actual purchase or face price of the ticket.

    Listings that violate this rule cannot be published.
